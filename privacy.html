Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026.

Shorts poster is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

Data the app accesses

When the owner connects their Google account, the app receives permission to upload videos to that account's YouTube channel (the youtube.upload scope). It does not read the owner's email, contacts, watch history or any other Google data.

Data storage

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server operated by the app or its developer. The app does not collect, sell, share or transfer any user data to third parties.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
