Shorts poster

Shorts poster is a personal tool that creates short animated videos on the owner's own computer and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel, on a schedule chosen by the owner.

What it does

  • Uploads videos that the owner created, with a title, description, tags and cover image, using the YouTube Data API (upload permission only).
  • Runs on the owner's computer. It is not a public service and has no other users.

See the privacy policy. Questions: open an issue.
